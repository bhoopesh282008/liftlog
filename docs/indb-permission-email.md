# Email: asking the INDB authors for permission

Send this from your own email. Draft only; nothing has been sent.

**To:** Lindsay.Jaacks@ed.ac.uk
**Subject:** Permission to use the Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB) in a free fitness app

---

Dear Dr Jaacks,

I'm building LiftLog, a free, non-commercial web app that helps people in
India track their workouts and protein intake. Accurate numbers for home-cooked
Indian dishes are the hardest part to get right, and the Indian Nutrient
Databank (Vijayakumar et al., *Current Developments in Nutrition*, 2024;
doi:10.1016/j.cdnut.2024.103790) is by far the best source I have found.

The INDB GitHub repository doesn't include a licence, so I've used it only
for my own personal tracking so far. I'd like to ask whether I may include it
in the public version of the app. Specifically:

- **What I'd use:** the per-serving energy, protein, carbohydrate and fat
  values for about 750 recipes. A further 261 rows are left out by my own
  checks; for example, recipes that count all the deep-frying oil as eaten.
- **How:** the values are shown in the app's food search. They're not sold,
  and they aren't offered as a separate download.
- **Credit:** a citation of the paper and a link to the repository in the
  app's About and Settings screens, in any wording you prefer.

If there are terms you'd like me to follow (a particular licence such as
CC BY, attribution wording, or limits on use), I'm happy to follow them.
If you'd rather I didn't use the data publicly, I'll keep it out of the
public app.

Thank you for making this work available. It's a real help to people
trying to eat well on Indian home food.

Kind regards,
Bhoopesh
https://liftlog-sooty.vercel.app
